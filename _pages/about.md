I am a PhD researcher in [Surgical & Interventional Engineering](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/bmeis/our-departments/surgical-interventional-engineering) at [King's College London](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/), and currently a Visiting Researcher in the [NeuroBionics Lab](https://biomedical.eng.unimelb.edu.au/john-neurobionics) at the [University of Melbourne](https://www.unimelb.edu.au). My research sits at the intersection of medical robotics, reinforcement learning, and neurovascular intervention.

I develop autonomous and AI-assisted systems for endovascular stroke treatment, using patient-specific vascular simulation environments to investigate safe robotic navigation through complex cerebrovascular anatomy. My work aims to advance intelligent robotic assistance for mechanical thrombectomy and improve the accessibility and scalability of stroke care.

I am supervised by [Thomas C. Booth](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/people/thomas-booth) and [Alejandro Granados](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/people/alejandro-granados). In 2021, I obtained an MEng in Electronic and Electrical Engineering (First Class Honours) from the [University of Bristol](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/).

## Research Interests

- Reinforcement learning
- Surgical robotics
- Endovascular intervention
- Autonomous navigation
